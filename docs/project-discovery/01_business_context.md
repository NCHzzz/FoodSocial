## Business Context

The product aims to solve food discovery and food sharing problems through a dedicated social platform focused entirely on food.

The platform should help users share food experiences, discover what to eat, and find more trusted food-related recommendations.

## Pain Points

### For users

- Existing platforms contain too much unrelated content
- Food discovery is fragmented across social apps, maps, and delivery apps
- It is difficult to know what dish is actually good in a specific place
- Social sharing around food is not structured enough for later retrieval and discovery
- Users may not trust highly seeded or overly promotional content

## Why Existing Solutions Are Not Enough

### Google Maps

- strong for place lookup
- weak for food-first social discovery and answering “what should I eat now?”

### TikTok

- strong for inspiration and short-form discovery
- weaker for trustworthy retrieval and structured food discovery

### General social media platforms

- too broad and noisy for focused food-interest discovery

## Product Opportunity

The product can differentiate by being:

- food-only
- community-driven
- more structured for discovery
- more trustworthy than high-noise, high-seeding channels
- eventually enhanced with AI-based food suggestions

Detailed AI-native and launch product direction live in `00_project_overview.md` and `04_functional_requirements.md`.

## Positioning Direction

The product is positioned as an **AI-native food social platform**.

This means the product should not only store and display food posts, but also help users answer food-related intent.

Detailed AI-native product direction lives in `00_project_overview.md`.

## Success Metrics

Primary launch success metric:

- establish a small group of real users who use the product repeatedly and find it genuinely useful

Public expansion gate after beta:

- should be based on active/retained real users and technical stability rather than a single metric

## Monetization Direction

- Controlled launch: no immediate monetization focus
- Later direction: keep a path open for multiple monetization models
- Primary long-term monetization priority: mixed model with affiliate / ordering commission as the main direction

## Assumptions

- A focused vertical social network for food can create better engagement than general-purpose social channels for this use case
- Users value relevance, trust, and discoverability over broad entertainment content in this domain

## Need Confirmation

- Primary KPI for launch
- Whether authenticity/trust is a brand promise that must become a measurable product objective
